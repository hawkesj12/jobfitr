    How it works

    You have a conversation. It asks about the work you want, where you can do it from, what you are good at and what you would turn down. Say just search at any point and it does. Then it searches, more than once, changing what it looks for based on what comes back, and reads the postings in full.

    Before you see anything, each job it chose is checked against what you said. A posting that breaks one of your dealbreakers is removed: remote only and the posting says on-site, pay that tops out under your floor, an employer you ruled out. You still see it, listed as ruled out with the posting's own words. Where a posting is silent, such as no salary listed, the card carries a warning instead. That can leave fewer than five, or none, and it says so.

    There is no fit score. A number would be easier to show and harder to trust, and it could not tell you why.

    Where the jobs come from

    Mostly companies' own hiring boards — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and friends — read directly, plus federal listings and, for local work, Google for Jobs and Adzuna. Most results link straight to the employer's posting. The rest — mainly local roles that reach jobfitr only through Adzuna — pass through that site first, and every card says which it is before you click. jobfitr takes no fee either way, and no employer can pay for a better answer.
