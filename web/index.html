    How it works

    You answer four questions in the box above. jobfitr scores every role in its pool against your answers — title match first, then the skills you said matter, minus anything you asked to avoid — and shows the best fits in order. Fit is relative to your own best match, not an absolute grade.

    Where the jobs come from

    Companies' own hiring boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and friends) plus a few open job APIs. Every result links straight to the employer's posting — jobfitr never sits between you and the application, and never takes a fee for it.
